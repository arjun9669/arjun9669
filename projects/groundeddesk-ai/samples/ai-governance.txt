AI Governance — Example Playbook

Purpose: This illustrative guidance explains how a small software team reviews AI use cases before launch. It is an educational sample and is not a company's internal policy.

Every AI use case should have a clearly named owner. The owner documents the user benefit, input-data categories, expected outputs, limitations and rollback procedure. Sensitive customer records must not be included in demos or public repositories.

For retrieval-augmented generation systems, document-grounded answers should provide explicit source citations. The evaluation plan should measure retrieval relevance, source attribution, unsupported-claim frequency and refusal behavior. Human reviewers should inspect a representative evaluation sample.

An AI deployment review checks access control, secret handling, content retention, audit logs and failure recovery. Production systems should have measurable service objectives and alerts for errors and excessive latency.

If an evaluation reveals unsupported answers or leaked sensitive content, deployment should be paused while the team investigates and improves the affected controls.
